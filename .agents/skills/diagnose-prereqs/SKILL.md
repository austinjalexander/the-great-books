# Diagnose readiness

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/PROFILE.md`, `docs/agents/tutoring.md`, progress, the target README, and recent learner evidence.

Distinguish required concepts from useful companion readings and later connections. Do not create an entrance requirement for Euclid Book I or another entry work; use an opening question instead. Recent relevant evidence can satisfy readiness without retesting.

For uncertain prerequisites, select up to five focused questions and a realistic total time budget, normally about ten minutes. Define critical criteria before testing: cite/reconstruct the necessary passage, justify a proof dependency, or parse the necessary forms. Keep solutions separate. Present one question at a time and record timing and aids as reported; do not coach during an unaided diagnostic.

Grade actual attempts using `grade-solution` or passage-specific feedback. With five questions, four sound answers and no critical gap suggest proceeding; two or three suggest a targeted refresh; fewer suggest returning to the specific prerequisite. Treat this as routing, not module completion. A critical gap can require repair regardless of total score.

Record questions, attempts, assistance, verdict, precise gap, and next reading/repair in the target's `learner-work.md`. Use an equivalent new task for reassessment. Weak current recall creates a review item; retain valid historical completion evidence rather than automatically unchecking a work.

# Review an attempt

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/PROFILE.md`, `docs/agents/tutoring.md`, the task and relevant primary passage. Consult a separate answer key only after the attempt. Verify the reasoning and cited text, not just the conclusion.

Return:

1. Verdict: correct, correct but unsupported, incomplete, or incorrect.
2. Earliest faulty or unsupported step, quoting the learner's actual line (or none).
3. Underlying concept or distinction; separate a local slip from a demonstrated conceptual gap.
4. One next action: a hint, step to redo, passage to inspect, or transfer question.
5. Check: a proof dependency, textual counterexample, grammar agreement, units, limiting case, or independent computation appropriate to the task.

Accept valid alternative demonstrations and interpretations supported by the text. Mark quotations or edition details you could not verify. Prefer staged hints; supply a worked answer when requested and label subsequent practice assisted. Record actual attempts and assistance in `learner-work.md`; one correct answer does not complete a work. Leave unaided assessments uncoached until submission.

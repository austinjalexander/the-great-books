# Resources

Canonical list of editions, translations, online primary texts, commentaries, and reference sites used across the curriculum. When a work-module's `README.md` points at "the primary edition," this is where the full entry lives.

## How to pick between options

- **Primary** = the edition to read from cover-to-cover for this module.
- **Alternative** = swap if the primary is not available or does not click.
- **Bilingual** = facing-page original (usually Loeb) for close study.
- **Commentary** = never read cover-to-cover; consult when puzzled.
- **Free online** = a legitimate, readable, citable digital text.

TAC's own editions have evolved over the years; where the college has published a "house manual" (Measurements Manual, Atomic Theory Manual, Latin stem-method materials) and no reliable substitute exists, that is flagged.

## General online primary-text archives

- [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/) — Greek and Latin texts with parsed morphology; best source for Plato, Aristotle, Homer, Herodotus, Thucydides, Virgil, Cicero, Tacitus.
- [CCEL — Christian Classics Ethereal Library](https://ccel.org/) — Augustine, Anselm, Aquinas (English), Athanasius, John Damascene, Luther.
- [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/) — *Summa Theologiae*, *Summa Contra Gentiles*, *Catechism*, *Catholic Encyclopedia*, full patristic translations.
- [The Latin Library](https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/) — raw Latin texts of Cicero, Horace, Livy, Virgil, Tacitus, Aquinas.
- [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) — older English translations of nearly everything in public domain.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) — scanned scholarly editions and older translations not on Gutenberg.
- [Sacred Texts](https://www.sacred-texts.com/) — backup for older translations of ancient / medieval religious texts.
- [Documenta Catholica Omnia](https://www.documentacatholicaomnia.eu/) — massive Latin archive of Fathers, Doctors, councils, encyclicals.
- [Vatican website](https://www.vatican.va/) — encyclicals (Leo XIII, Pius X, Pius XI, Pius XII, JPII) in multiple languages.

## Bibles

- Primary: **RSV–2CE (Ignatius Bible)** — the translation TAC uses in the Catholic edition.
- Alternative: **Douay-Rheims / Challoner** — close to the Latin Vulgate; useful for reading Aquinas.
- Alternative: **ESV Catholic Edition** — when readability matters and RSV-2CE feels stilted.
- Bilingual: **Nova Vulgata** + RSV-2CE side by side for passages where Aquinas's Latin matters.
- Reference: **Haydock Commentary**, **Navarre Bible**, **Ignatius Catholic Study Bible** — Catholic commentaries.

## Plato

- Primary: **Hackett** single-dialogue volumes (Grube/Reeve for *Republic*, Nehamas-Woodruff for *Phaedrus* and *Symposium*, Cooper ed. for *Complete Works*).
- Alternative: Jowett translation on Perseus (free, older).
- Bilingual: **Loeb Classical Library** (Lamb, Bury, Fowler).
- Commentary: Cornford — *Plato's Cosmology* (*Timaeus*); Cornford — *Plato's Theory of Knowledge* (*Theaetetus* / *Sophist*); Burnet — Greek text with notes (*Euthyphro*, *Apology*, *Crito*, *Phaedo*).

## Aristotle

- Primary: **Bollingen / Princeton complete Aristotle, ed. Barnes, 2 vols.** — standard revised Oxford translations.
- Alternative: **Hackett** volumes (Irwin for *Nicomachean Ethics*, Reeve for *Metaphysics* and *Politics*, Apostle for *Physics*).
- Bilingual: **Loeb**.
- Commentary: **Aquinas's commentaries** (see below) are the TAC-preferred route into Aristotle.
- Translation for *Organon*: Ackrill (*Categories*, *De Interpretatione*), Smith (*Prior Analytics*), Barnes (*Posterior Analytics*), Smith (*Topics*) — all in the Oxford Clarendon series.

## Aquinas

- Primary *Summa Theologiae*: **Fathers of the English Dominican Province** translation, widely available via New Advent, Christian Classics, and reprints. For serious work, the **Benziger Brothers** 5-vol. or **Christian Classics** 5-vol. hardbacks.
- Modern *Summa*: **Aquinas Institute (Latin-English parallel)** — the best scholarly English translation available today; slow but worth it.
- *Summa Contra Gentiles*: **University of Notre Dame / Hanover House** translation (Pegis, Anderson, Bourke, O'Neil).
- Shorter works: **Hackett** *On Being and Essence* (Maurer); **Aquinas Institute** bilingual editions of the Aristotle commentaries (*In Phys.*, *In Metaph.*, *In NE*, *In De Anima*, *In Post. An.*).
- Free Latin: [Corpus Thomisticum](https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/) — the complete Latin corpus of Aquinas.
- Reference: Gilson — *The Christian Philosophy of St. Thomas Aquinas*.

## Augustine

- Primary *Confessions*: **Chadwick (Oxford World's Classics)** or **Boulding (New City Press)**.
- Primary *City of God*: **Bettenson (Penguin)** or **Dyson (Cambridge Texts)**.
- Shorter works: **New City Press — Works of Saint Augustine** series (authoritative modern translations).
- Free: CCEL has Schaff-era translations of nearly everything.

## Anselm, Athanasius, Damascene

- Anselm: **Davies & Evans (Oxford World's Classics)** or **Charlesworth (Notre Dame)** for *Proslogion*.
- Athanasius: **St. Vladimir's Seminary Press** — *On the Incarnation* (Behr translation with Lewis foreword).
- John Damascene: CCEL / Schaff, *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers* Second Series vol. IX — *An Exact Exposition*.

## Homer

- Primary *Iliad*: **Lattimore** (Chicago) — the translation TAC has historically favored.
- Alternative: **Fagles (Penguin)** for readability.
- Bilingual: Loeb (Wyatt / Murray).
- Primary *Odyssey*: **Fagles** or **Lattimore**.
- Commentary: Willcock — *Iliad* student edition.

## Greek tragedy and comedy

- Primary: **Chicago "Complete Greek Tragedies"** series (Grene, Lattimore, eds.) — Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides.
- Alternative: **Penguin Classics** (Vellacott, Fagles).
- Aristophanes: **Arrowsmith / Lattimore** (Chicago) or **Henderson (Loeb)**.

## Herodotus, Thucydides

- Herodotus: **Landmark Herodotus, ed. Strassler** (Pantheon) — indispensable maps.
- Thucydides: **Landmark Thucydides, ed. Strassler** (Free Press).
- Alternatives: Grene (Herodotus), Hammond (Thucydides).

## Virgil, Lucretius, Horace, Cicero, Livy, Tacitus

- Virgil *Aeneid*: **Mandelbaum** or **Fagles**; bilingual Loeb.
- Lucretius: **Smith / Martin Ferguson Smith (Hackett)**; alternatives Stallings, Bailey.
- Horace: Loeb.
- Cicero *On Duties*: **Griffin & Atkins (Cambridge Texts)**.
- Livy: **Landmark Livy, ed. Strassler** or de Sélincourt (Penguin).
- Tacitus *Annals*: **Woodman (Hackett)** or Grant (Penguin).
- Plutarch *Lives*: **Dryden translation (Modern Library)**; alternative Perrin (Loeb).

## Boethius, Epictetus

- Boethius *Consolation*: **Relihan (Hackett)** or Watts (Penguin).
- Epictetus *Manual* (*Enchiridion*): **Hard (Oxford World's Classics)** or Long.

## Dante

- Primary: **Sinclair, Oxford, 3 vols.** — prose crib with Italian facing.
- Alternative *verse*: **Hollander** (Princeton) — annotated.
- Alternative: **Ciardi** or **Musa** (Penguin).
- Commentary: Singleton (Princeton) — the standard English-language commentary; very long.

## Chaucer, Pearl Poet, Spenser

- Chaucer: **Riverside Chaucer, 3rd ed.** (standard).
- Pearl Poet *Sir Gawain*: **Tolkien & Gordon** (scholarly) or **Armitage** (accessible verse).
- Spenser *Faerie Queene*: **A. C. Hamilton, Longman Annotated English Poets**.

## Cervantes

- Primary: **Grossman (Ecco)** — modern, readable.
- Alternative: Rutherford (Penguin).

## Shakespeare

- Primary: **Arden Shakespeare (Third Series)** — extensive textual notes, scholarly apparatus.
- Alternative: **Folger** (free online, good introductions).
- Alternative: **Riverside Shakespeare** — one-volume complete.

## Milton, Corneille, Racine, Goethe

- Milton *Paradise Lost*: **Fowler (Longman Annotated English Poets)**.
- Corneille *Le Cid*: Cairncross (Penguin) or free public-domain verse translations.
- Racine *Phèdre*: **Edwards** or **Hughes** verse translations.
- Goethe *Faust*: **Kaufmann (Anchor) Part I**; Arndt for both parts.

## Euclid and the Green Lion Press

- Primary *Elements*: **Heath translation** — either the three-volume Dover (cheap, with full commentary) or the one-volume Green Lion.
- **Green Lion Press** — publishes TAC-friendly editions of Euclid, Apollonius, Archimedes, Ptolemy, Kepler, Newton. This press is the single most useful source for TAC's mathematics and natural-science syllabus.

## Latin — learning the language

- **Henle Latin** — First–Fourth Year. Used by many classical schools; close in spirit to TAC's stem method.
- **Hans Ørberg — *Lingua Latina per se Illustrata*** (vols. I *Familia Romana* and II *Roma Aeterna*) — the best immersion text; excellent complement to TAC's reading selections.
- **Wheelock's Latin** — conventional grammar-based textbook.
- **Moreland & Fleischer — *Latin: An Intensive Course*** (UC Press) — fast, rigorous, used in summer intensives.
- **Allen & Greenough — *New Latin Grammar*** (Dover) — reference.
- **Gildersleeve & Lodge — *Latin Grammar*** (Dover) — reference.
- **Lewis & Short — *A Latin Dictionary*** (free on Perseus).
- Online drill: [The Latin Library](https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/), [Perseus Greek Word Study Tool](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/morph), [Memrise / Anki decks for Wheelock and Ørberg].
- Nesfield *Aids to the Study and Composition of English* — scanned copies freely available on Internet Archive.

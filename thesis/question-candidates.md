# Candidate Thesis Questions

> Append-only through Sophomore year. Log candidates as they surface in reading and discussion. Volume is the goal; do not trim early. In Sophomore year, review and promote three strongest candidates to the "Top three" section. In Junior year, pick one.

## Instructions for adding a candidate

For each candidate, record:

- **Q** — the question in one sentence.
- **Source work(s)** — which work(s) triggered it, with passage citations.
- **Rationale** — one sentence on why the question is interesting and what would count as answering it.
- **Connections** — other works (already read or anticipated) where the question reappears.
- **Date logged**.

# Senior Thesis

The Senior Thesis is the culminating work of the TAC curriculum: a substantial written composition on a question the student has framed under the guidance of a tutor, defended before a committee of examiners.

This folder is alive from Freshman year, not Senior year. Candidate questions accumulate from the first seminar. By Senior year, one of them has become a 40–80 page defended essay.

## Layout

- [`question-candidates.md`](question-candidates.md) — running list of candidate questions, with the work they arose from, passage citations, and a one-sentence rationale. Append-only through Sophomore year; later consolidated into a "Top three" section.
- [`outline.md`](outline.md) — one-page outline drafted in Junior year, revised throughout Senior year. Scaffolded from [`../templates/thesis-outline-template.md`](../templates/thesis-outline-template.md).
- [`bibliography.md`](bibliography.md) — primary sources first, secondary second; consistent citation style (Chicago notes-and-bibliography or MLA, your choice — but consistent).
- [`notes/`](notes/) — working notes by source.
- [`drafts/`](drafts/) — one file per major draft (`draft-1.md`, `draft-2.md`, `draft-final.md`). Drafts are append-only: never overwrite an earlier draft.
- [`defense-prep.md`](defense-prep.md) — the ten questions the student most expects the committee to ask, each with a short answer tied to a specific passage in the thesis.

## Year-by-year discipline

See [`.agents/skills/thesis-advisor/SKILL.md`](../.agents/skills/thesis-advisor/SKILL.md) for the full staged guidance.

- **Freshman**: log ≥ 20 candidate questions. Volume is the goal. Do not trim.
- **Sophomore**: refine to three strongest candidates, each with a one-paragraph rationale.
- **Junior**: pick one. Draft the one-page outline. Compile the bibliography.
- **Senior**: draft by outline section; revise in full passes; prepare defense.

## Standards

The question, at the end of the four years, should be one the authors in the program themselves frame. "What is tonal music?" reframed as "Is tonal music natural?" is the kind of move. A thesis that can only be stated in a commentator's vocabulary is the wrong thesis.

# Year 3 — Junior

*Summa Theologiae* I on sacred doctrine, God, and law; Aristotle's *Ethics* and *Politics*; the new science (Descartes, Galileo, Newton first pass); analytic geometry and the beginnings of calculus; Music as its own subject track; a sprawling seminar through early-modern political philosophy, Shakespeare, and the English empiricists. The Latin sequence ends; it is replaced by Music.

# 2-Philosophy — Aristotle's Moral and Political Philosophy

*Nicomachean Ethics* and *Politics* read together as Aristotle's single argument from the human good to the human polity.

**Syllabus citation** (Junior, Philosophy): Aristotle — *Nicomachean Ethics*, *Politics*.

**Primary editions**: Irwin (Hackett) for *Nicomachean Ethics*; Reeve (Hackett) for *Politics*. Also: Aquinas Institute bilingual Aquinas commentaries *In NE* and *In Pol.* See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#aristotle).

## Reading order

1. `01-aristotle-nicomachean-ethics/` — all ten books.
2. `02-aristotle-politics/` — all eight books.

## Companion readings across the program

- 1-Philosophy — Plato *Meno*, *Gorgias*, *Republic* on virtue and justice.
- 2-Philosophy — *De Anima* on the faculties the *Ethics* distinguishes.
- 3-Theology — *Summa Theologiae* I–II on law presupposes this reading.
- 3-Seminar — Aquinas *On Kingship*, Machiavelli, Hobbes, Locke, Rousseau all argue with Aristotle on politics.

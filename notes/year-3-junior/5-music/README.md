# 5-Music

Zuckerkandl on the phenomenology of tonal music; Boethius on ratios; Mozart's piano sonatas as the worked example; Gustin on tonality; Kalkavage on the measurement of tones.

**Syllabus citation** (Junior, Music): Zuckerkandl — *The Sense of Music*; Boethius — *On Music*; Mozart — *Sonatas*; Gustin — *Tonality*; Kalkavage — *On the Measurement of Tones*.

**Primary materials**: Princeton for Zuckerkandl; Bower (Yale) for Boethius; Henle Urtext scores for Mozart (plus a recording of your choice — Brendel, Uchida, Hewitt). See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#music).

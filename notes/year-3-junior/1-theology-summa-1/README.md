# 1-Theology — *Summa Theologiae* I

Selected treatises from the *Prima Pars* and the *Prima Secundae*: sacred doctrine itself, God, law.

**Syllabus citation** (Junior, Theology): St. Thomas Aquinas — *Summa Theologiae*: On Sacred Doctrine, On God, On Law.

**Primary edition**: Fathers of the English Dominican Province translation (free at New Advent); Aquinas Institute bilingual for close Latin study. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#aquinas).

## Reading order

1. `01-st-on-sacred-doctrine/` — *ST* I, Q. 1.
2. `02-st-on-god/` — *ST* I, Qq. 2–26 (existence, simplicity, perfection, goodness, infinity, knowledge, will, justice and mercy, providence).
3. `03-st-on-law/` — *ST* I–II, Qq. 90–108 (essence of law; kinds of law; eternal, natural, human, divine; the Old and New Law).

Each of these three clusters is a module with its own `README.md`, `notes.md`, `discussion.md`, and `reflection.md`. Given the length, consider splitting a cluster further into sub-module folders by treatise (e.g., `02-st-on-god/01-existence-simplicity/`) if the weight warrants it.

## Companion readings across the program

- 1-Theology — Scripture (the *Summa*'s first premise).
- 1-Philosophy — Aristotle *Organon* (the structure of demonstration the *Summa* uses).
- 2-Theology — Augustine and the Fathers (Aquinas's continual interlocutors).
- 2-Philosophy — Aristotle *Physics*, *De Anima* (built into the language of the *Summa*).
- 3-Philosophy — *Nicomachean Ethics* directly informs *ST* I–II on law and the moral life.
- 4-Theology — *Summa* II on Trinity, Sacraments, Christ continues the sequence.

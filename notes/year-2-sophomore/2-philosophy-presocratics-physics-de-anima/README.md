# 2-Philosophy — Pre-Socratics, *Physics*, *De Anima*

Pre-Socratic fragments read as the prehistory of natural philosophy; Aristotle's *Physics* on motion, nature, and cause; *De Anima* on soul and faculties.

**Syllabus citation** (Sophomore, Philosophy): Pre-Socratic Philosophers — Fragments; Aristotle — *Physics*; Aristotle — *De Anima*.

**Primary editions**: Kirk-Raven-Schofield *Presocratic Philosophers* for fragments; Apostle or Barnes for *Physics*; Hamlyn or Shields for *De Anima*. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#aristotle).

## Reading order

1. `01-presocratic-fragments/` — Thales through the Atomists.
2. `02-aristotle-physics/` — all eight books.
3. `03-aristotle-de-anima/` — all three books.

## Companion readings across the program

- 1-Philosophy — Aristotle *Organon* supplies the method; *Posterior Analytics* I.2 frames what *Physics* is doing.
- 2-Natural-Science — Aquinas *On the Principles of Nature* directly rests on the *Physics*.
- 3-Philosophy — *Nicomachean Ethics* presupposes the account of the soul in *De Anima*.
- 4-Philosophy — *Physics* is revisited in Senior year with *Metaphysics* and *On Being and Essence*; the module here cross-links to the Senior-year revisit.

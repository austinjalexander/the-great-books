# 1-Theology — The Fathers and the Incarnation

Augustine on hermeneutics, grace, and the two cities; the Council of Orange; Athanasius on the Incarnation; Anselm and Gaunilo on the ontological argument; John Damascene's systematic compendium.

**Syllabus citation** (Sophomore, Theology): Augustine — *On Christian Doctrine*, *On the Spirit and the Letter*, *On Nature and Grace*, *On the Predestination of the Saints*, *On the Gift of Perseverance*, *City of God*; Council of Orange — *Canons*; Athanasius — *On the Incarnation*; Gaunilo — *On Behalf of the Fool*; Anselm — *Proslogion*, *Reply to Gaunilo*; John Damascene — *An Exact Exposition of the Orthodox Faith*.

**Primary editions**: New City Press Augustine; St. Vladimir's Seminary Press Athanasius; Oxford World's Classics Anselm; Schaff NPNF2.IX for Damascene. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#augustine).

- 2-Seminar — Augustine's *Confessions* is the seminar companion to the theology cluster.

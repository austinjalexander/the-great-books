# 5-Language — Latin II

Second year of Latin: harder stem-method readings, Martin of Denmark on the modes of signifying, and substantive passages from Horace, Cicero, Aquinas, and the Canon of the Mass.

**Syllabus citation** (Sophomore, Language): Latin Readings According to the "Stem Method" (Sophomore Year); Martin of Denmark — *Tractas De Modis Significandi*; Horace, Cicero — selections; St. Thomas Aquinas — selections; Canon of the Mass.

**Primary materials**: TAC stem-method reader; Loeb bilingual Horace and Cicero; Aquinas Institute bilingual editions; Missale Romanum for the Canon of the Mass. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#latin--learning-the-language).

## Companion readings across the program

- 1-Language — Latin I is a strict prerequisite.
- 2-Theology — Augustine and Anselm are read in English here but paralleled with Latin passages from the corpus.
- 3-Theology and 4-Theology — the Latin skill built here is what makes the *Summa* readable in its original form.

# 4-Mathematics — Euclid

Euclid's *Elements*. One year of geometry, number theory, and the method that underlies every mathematical work TAC reads after it.

**Syllabus citation** (Freshman, Mathematics): Euclid — *Elements*.

**Primary edition**: Heath translation, either the three-volume Dover with full commentary or the one-volume Green Lion. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#euclid-and-the-green-lion-press).

## Reading order

1. [`01-euclid-elements-book-1/`](01-euclid-elements-book-1/) — definitions, postulates, common notions; I.1–I.48 (including the Pythagorean theorem at I.47).
2. `02-euclid-elements-book-2/` — geometric algebra.
3. `03-euclid-elements-book-3/` — circles.
4. `04-euclid-elements-book-4/` — inscribed and circumscribed regular figures.
5. `05-euclid-elements-book-5/` — theory of proportion (Eudoxan).
6. `06-euclid-elements-book-6/` — similar figures.
7. `07-euclid-elements-selections-7-13/` — number theory (VII–IX), incommensurables (X), solid geometry and the five Platonic solids (XI–XIII).

## The reference module

Book I is the canonical reference module for the whole repository, the Great-Books analogue of `01-algebra` in the physics repo. When authoring another module, inspect [`01-euclid-elements-book-1/`](01-euclid-elements-book-1/) end-to-end — including its separate key — for layout. Learners should read the primary text and prompts first, keeping `solutions.md` closed until after attempts.

## Companion readings across the program

- 1-Philosophy — Aristotle *Posterior Analytics* I.2 on the structure of demonstrative science; Euclid is the great example.
- 2-Mathematics — Ptolemy and Apollonius both presuppose Euclid throughout.
- 3-Mathematics — Viète and Descartes's *Geometry* both sit in self-conscious relation to Euclid.
- 3-Natural-Science — Galileo and Newton write in Euclidean style.
- 4-Mathematics — Lobachevski's *Geometrical Researches* begins from the question of whether Euclid's fifth postulate is necessary.

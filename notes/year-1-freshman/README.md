# Year 1 — Freshman

The ground-floor year. Greek origins of philosophy, mathematics, and natural science; the Bible read whole; English composition; Latin begun. Seminar runs on Greek poetry, history, and philosophy.

See [`docs/CURRICULUM.md`](../../docs/CURRICULUM.md#year-1--freshman) for the complete reading list and [`STUDY_PLAN.md`](../../STUDY_PLAN.md#year-1--freshman-900-hrs) for pacing.

## Subject tracks

- [`1-theology-bible/`](1-theology-bible/) — The Holy Bible.
- [`2-philosophy-plato-organon/`](2-philosophy-plato-organon/) — Plato's early and middle dialogues; Porphyry's *Isagoge*; Aristotle's *Organon*.
- [`3-natural-science-life/`](3-natural-science-life/) — Aristotle on living things; Harvey, Galen, Linnaeus, Fabre, Mendel; hydrostatics from Archimedes and Pascal.
- [`4-mathematics-euclid/`](4-mathematics-euclid/) — *Elements*.
- [`5-language-latin-1/`](5-language-latin-1/) — English composition (Nesfield); Latin stem-method primer and readings.
- [`6-seminar/`](6-seminar/) — Homer, Plato, Aeschylus, Sophocles, Herodotus, Plutarch, Aristotle, Euripides, Thucydides, Aristophanes.

## Compositions and thesis-thread

- Five essays this year, under [`../../compositions/year-1/`](../../compositions/year-1/).
- Thesis-candidate questions accumulate under [`../../thesis/question-candidates.md`](../../thesis/question-candidates.md). Aim for at least 20 by year-end.

## Concurrency

Run all six tracks every week. The works are arranged to illuminate each other — Aristotle's *Poetics* in Seminar and Aristotle's *Posterior Analytics* in Philosophy speak to each other in the same month, not sequentially.

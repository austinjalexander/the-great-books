# 1-Theology — The Holy Bible

The Bible read as primary theological source. At TAC the reading is selective but extensive; in this repo each cluster of books has its own module.

**Syllabus citation** (Freshman, Theology): "Theology | The Holy Bible."

**Primary edition**: RSV-2CE (Ignatius Bible). Alternative and bilingual editions in [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#bibles).

## Reading order

1. `01-pentateuch/` — Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy.
2. `02-historical-books/` — Joshua through 2 Maccabees (selections).
3. `03-wisdom/` — Job, Psalms (selections), Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song, Wisdom, Sirach.
4. `04-prophets/` — Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, the minor prophets (selections).
5. `05-gospel-matthew/` — Matthew.
6. `06-gospel-mark/` — Mark.
7. `07-gospel-luke/` — Luke.
8. `08-gospel-john/` — John.
9. `09-acts/` — Acts of the Apostles.
10. `10-pauline-epistles/` — Romans through Philemon.
11. `11-hebrews/` — Hebrews.
12. `12-catholic-epistles-revelation/` — James through Revelation.

## Companion readings across the program

- Plato *Phaedo* (Freshman Philosophy) — immortality of the soul; Platonic vs. biblical anthropology.
- Augustine and the Fathers (Sophomore Theology) — the standard second-order readers.
- Aquinas *Summa* (Junior and Senior Theology) — Scripture read as the first source of sacred doctrine.

# 2-Philosophy — Metaphysics

Aristotle's *Physics* revisited with metaphysical intent; *Metaphysics* read whole; Aquinas's short *On Being and Essence* as the most compact statement of Thomistic metaphysics.

**Syllabus citation** (Senior, Philosophy): Aristotle — *Physics*; Aristotle — *Metaphysics*; St. Thomas Aquinas — *On Being and Essence*.

**Primary editions**: Apostle or Barnes for *Physics*; Reeve (Hackett) or Barnes for *Metaphysics*; Maurer (Hackett) for *On Being and Essence*. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#aristotle).

## Reading order

1. `01-aristotle-physics-senior-revisit/` — the philosophical pass; cross-links to the Sophomore natural-philosophy pass.
2. `02-aristotle-metaphysics/` — all fourteen books.
3. `03-aquinas-on-being-and-essence/` — the entire short treatise.

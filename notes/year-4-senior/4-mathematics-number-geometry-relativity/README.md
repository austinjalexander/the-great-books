# 4-Mathematics — Foundations, Non-Euclidean Geometry, Relativity

Dedekind's construction of the real numbers; Lobachevski on parallels; Einstein on Special and General Relativity; Oersted and Ampère as electromagnetic context.

**Syllabus citation** (Senior, Mathematics): Dedekind — *Essay on the Theory of Numbers*; Lobachevski — *Geometrical Researches on the Theory of Parallels*; Einstein — *Relativity: The Special and the General Theory*; Oersted, Ampère, *et alia*.

**Primary editions**: Beman / Dover for Dedekind; Halsted / Dover for Lobachevski; Crown or Dover for Einstein. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#galileo-newton-kepler-descartes-geometry-huygens-faraday-maxwell-einstein).

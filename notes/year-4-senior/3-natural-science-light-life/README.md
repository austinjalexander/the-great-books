# 3-Natural-Science — Light, Electromagnetism, Life

Newton revisited with *Opticks* alongside *Principia*; Huygens and Young on light; Gilbert, Faraday, Maxwell on electromagnetism; Darwin and the biological tradition from Jenkins and Mivart through Mendel (revisit), Schrödinger, Mayr, Watson and Crick, Monod, Jonas, Eldredge and Gould, Harold, Polanyi, Kass; Aquinas's *Summa Contra Gentiles* and metaphysical commentaries as philosophical companions; Aristotle *Metaphysics* reread as natural-science companion; Augustine *Literal Interpretation of Genesis*; a house manual on electricity and magnetism.

**Syllabus citation** (Senior, Natural Sciences): see [`../../../docs/CURRICULUM.md`](../../../docs/CURRICULUM.md#4-natural-science--light-electromagnetism-life).

**Primary editions**: Cohen-Whitman for *Principia*; Dover reprint for *Opticks*; Thompson (Dover) for Huygens; Mottelay (Dover) for Gilbert; Dover for Faraday; *The Scientific Papers of James Clerk Maxwell*; Norton Critical or Penguin for Darwin; translations listed in [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md) for later biology; Aquinas Institute for *SCG* and commentaries.

# 1-Theology — *Summa Theologiae* II

Selected treatises: the Trinity, the Sacraments, the Passion of Christ.

**Syllabus citation** (Senior, Theology): St. Thomas Aquinas — *Summa Theologiae*: On the Trinity, On the Sacraments, On the Passion of Christ.

**Primary edition**: Fathers of the English Dominican Province translation; Aquinas Institute bilingual for close Latin work. See [`RESOURCES.md`](../../../RESOURCES.md#aquinas).

## Reading order

1. `01-st-on-the-trinity/` — *ST* I, Qq. 27–43.
2. `02-st-on-the-sacraments/` — *ST* III, Qq. 60–83 (selections).
3. `03-st-on-the-passion-of-christ/` — *ST* III, Qq. 46–52.

## Companion readings across the program

- 3-Theology — *Summa Theologiae* I on Sacred Doctrine, God, and Law.
- 2-Theology — Augustine *On the Trinity* (if the student takes the optional detour); Damascene's *Exact Exposition*.
- 4-Philosophy — *On Being and Essence*, *Metaphysics*: philosophical backbone of these treatises.

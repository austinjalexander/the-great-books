# 5-Seminar — Modernity: Literature, Economics, Psychology, Theology

The modern world, taken seriously. Tolstoy; Smith's economics; Kant in two works; Goethe's *Faust*; Hegel in two works; Feuerbach; Austen; Tocqueville on democracy and on the French Revolution; Twain; Marx and Engels extensively; Ibsen; Dostoyevsky; Nietzsche; Kierkegaard; Cather; Flaubert; Newman; Conrad; twentieth-century poetry (Keats, Hopkins, Stevens, Eliot); Joyce; Freud; Jung; Heidegger; Faulkner; Aquinas on the division of the sciences; modern encyclicals (Pius X, Leo XIII × 2, Pius XII, Pius XI); O'Connor; Plato *Phaedrus*; John Paul II's *Veritatis Splendor*.

## Companion readings across the program

- 3-Seminar — Kant, Locke, Rousseau, the Founding documents are all revisited here in conversation with moderns.
- 3-Theology — the *Summa* is the theological standard the modern encyclicals maintain.
- 4-Natural-Science — Darwin and modern biology are alive in the seminar conversations on Marx, Freud, Jonas.

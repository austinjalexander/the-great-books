# Compositions

Short essays written across Years 1–3. Scaffold each from [`../templates/composition-template.md`](../templates/composition-template.md). Use [`../.agents/skills/grade-composition/SKILL.md`](../.agents/skills/grade-composition/SKILL.md) to review on the rubric before calling an essay done.

- [`year-1/`](year-1/) — five essays.
- [`year-2/`](year-2/) — four essays.
- [`year-3/`](year-3/) — two longer essays.

Senior year's writing is the thesis itself, under [`../thesis/`](../thesis/).

## File-naming

`NN-short-descriptive-slug.md` within each year, e.g. `01-justice-in-republic-iv.md`, `02-plutarch-on-alcibiades.md`. The `NN` orders them through the year.

## Pace

Essays are spaced through the year, not clumped at term-end. Freshman: roughly one per 6 weeks. Sophomore: roughly one per 7–8 weeks. Junior: two longer essays, one per semester.
